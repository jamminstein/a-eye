# The 50 eyes: Kling motion prompts

Negative prompt (all clips):

> morphing, warping eyes, melting, flicker, jitter, extra pupils, changing iris colour, deformed pupil, extra or merged eyelashes, plastic or waxy skin, airbrushed, cartoon, CGI, 3D render, illustration, oversaturated, sharpening halos, zoom, camera shake, pan, tilt, text, watermark, subtitles, speaking, lip movement, head turning away, hands

## eye-01: Dawn, chestnut (macro)

*a woman in her early thirties of southern Italian descent*

Steady gaze into the lens. The pupil slowly opens as the room darkens a little. One unhurried blink around the fourth second, lashes settling after it. The eye then drifts a few degrees toward the window and comes back to the lens. Locked-off tripod macro shot, no zoom, no pan, no camera movement, natural skin micro-movement.
